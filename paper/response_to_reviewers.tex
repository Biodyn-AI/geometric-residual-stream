\section{Editor}

\reviewer{Please ensure the results are accurately reported, any overstated
conclusions are rewritten and the limitations of the work fully explained.}

We have (i) added absolute AUROC and AUPRC to every results table, including the
cell-type stratification table; (ii) added a leakage-resistant grouped
cross-validation analysis that honestly bounds the signal---it collapses under
leave-both-out cross-validation; (iii) frozen a confirmatory pipeline applied
once to held-out domains; (iv) recalibrated the title, abstract, contributions,
Discussion and Conclusions to ``incremental signal for retrospective edge
prioritization''; and (v) expanded the Limitations from five to seven points.
Specifics appear under the reviewer responses below.

\section{Reviewer 1}

\rc{1. Calibrate core claims more conservatively.}
We recalibrated throughout. The \textbf{title} now reads ``Carries Incremental
Gene-Regulatory Signal'' (was ``Encodes Gene Regulatory Structure''). The
abstract, the five-point contribution list, Discussion and Conclusions now use
``incremental regulatory-relevant signal for retrospective edge prioritization''
and state explicitly that the signal is statistical enrichment, not a
stand-alone classifier. The recalibration is grounded in the new grouped-CV
results (point~3), not wording alone.

\rc{2. Make exploratory vs.\ confirmatory analyses explicit.}
\textit{New experiment (WP5, cycle47).} A new Methods subsection states that
representation choices were selected on kidney and immune. We then \textbf{froze}
a single pre-specified pipeline (centered cosine, bundle L0--11, stacking; no
per-domain tuning) and applied it once to the held-out confirmatory domains.
Result: lung $\Delta$AUROC $+0.032\pm0.006$, external lung $+0.027\pm0.005$ (all
three seeds with bootstrap CIs above zero) under edge CV; $+0.0016$ and $+0.0009$
under leave-both-out. Reported in a new Results subsection and table.

\rc{3. Strengthen leakage/generalization stress tests.}
\textit{New experiment (WP1, cycle43; WP13, cycle53).} We re-evaluated under
leave-TF-out, leave-target-out and leave-both-out grouped CV (new Methods
subsection; new Table~``Geometric signal under grouped, leakage-resistant
cross-validation''). The signal is fully robust to leave-TF-out and
leave-target-out (delta comparable to, or larger than, edge CV) but
\textbf{collapses to near-zero under leave-both-out} ($+0.001$ to $+0.003$); the
confound baseline also drops to AUROC $\approx0.50$ there. This is now reported as
the honest ceiling of out-of-entity generalization, in Results, Discussion and as
Limitation~3. We additionally extended the stress test to the compact stacking
model (WP13): its advantage over the best single branch is preserved under
edge-level CV but, like the underlying signal, shrinks under leave-both-out.

\rc{4. Expand and clarify negative-edge robustness.}
\textit{New experiment (WP2, cycle44).} A new Methods subsection states the exact
protocol (single rejection-sampled draw for the primary set). We additionally
generated \textbf{degree-matched} and \textbf{expression-matched} harder
negatives, each repeated for eight independent draws. The signal is stable across
draws (SD $\le 0.042$) and does not weaken under harder negatives---in the lung
domains degree-matched negatives \emph{increase} $\Delta$AUROC. New
Table~``Robustness to negative-edge construction''.

\rc{5. Temper the scGPT vs.\ Geneformer comparison, or make representations
comparable.}
We chose to \textbf{make the extraction comparable} (option~a).
\textit{New experiment (WP6, cycle51).} We extracted Geneformer per-layer
residual-stream activations from real forward passes over single cells (18-layer
model, official rank-value tokenization) and applied the identical geometric
pipeline to both models. Under matched extraction the apparent Geneformer
advantage largely disappears (scGPT ahead in kidney; remaining gaps
$+0.009$--$0.023$). New Results subsection and Table~``Cross-model comparison
under matched per-layer extraction''; the original table's caption now flags its
asymmetry; architectural-superiority language has been removed.

\rc{6. Improve reporting of absolute performance, uncertainty, multiplicity.}
\textit{New experiments (WP3, cycle45; WP4, cycle46; WP12, cycle52).} Absolute
AUROC and AUPRC are now reported in \emph{every} results table---including the
cell-type stratification table---and in a full per-layer supplementary panel. A
new Methods subsection ``Uncertainty decomposition and multiplicity'' states the
bootstrap unit (edges, or gene clusters under grouped CV), decomposes variance
into within-run and across-seed components (across-seed dominates, 64--83\%), and
applies Benjamini--Hochberg correction across the 148-comparison grid (42 survive
$q<0.05$, including all headline results).

\rc{7. Add operational detail for reproducibility.}
A new Methods paragraph ``Implementation detail'' specifies the
logistic-regression hyperparameters, in-fold standardization, randomized-SVD PCA
configuration, cell-sampling policy, coverage filters and gene/cell aggregation.
Every new analysis has a dedicated Methods subsection, and
\texttt{implementation/README.md} lists reproduction commands for all new cycles.

\rc{8. Keep biological interpretation clearly labeled as interpretation.}
The CD8$^+$ T-cell paragraph now frames the account explicitly as ``a
biologically plausible hypothesis $\ldots$ offered as interpretation rather than
as an established mechanism'' and lists alternative explanations (cell-type
abundance, library complexity, annotation density) that are not excluded.

\section{Reviewer 2}

\rc{1. Report actual AUROC, not just delta.}
Done (WP3, WP12). Every results table now reports baseline-only and
geometry-augmented absolute AUROC. Baseline AUROC ranges $0.50$--$0.65$; the
geometry-augmented AUROC ($0.58$--$0.69$) is always above both $0.5$ and the
baseline, so the deltas are genuine improvements over a meaningful starting
point.

\rc{2. Actual AUROC for usability; envision how to sift the signal.}
\textit{New experiment (WP10, cycle49).} A new Results subsection reports top-$k$
retrieval: the geometry-augmented model enriches true edges $1.6$--$2.1\times$
over prevalence among the top~50 candidates, and we report a calibrated operating
point (precision/recall). We state plainly that the modest absolute AUROC means
the signal is an evidence channel for re-ranking, not a stand-alone caller, and
the Discussion gives a concrete pipeline (symmetric score for edge presence,
asymmetric score for orientation, ensembling with GENIE3).

\rc{3. Apply the refined methodology to the earlier results.}
Done (WP3/WP7). The refined pipeline (centered cosine, PCA, bundle) is now applied
uniformly to kidney and immune as well; it improves those domains too (kidney
$\Delta$AUROC $+0.076\to+0.122$; immune $+0.024\to+0.042$). A new unified-method
table replaces the old two-panel table; the Results state that the refinement is
a uniform improvement, not a lung-specific rescue.

\rc{4. AUROC can be misleading under class imbalance---report AUPRC.}
Done. AUPRC and $\Delta$AUPRC are reported in all results tables and the
supplementary panel, and precision/recall@$k$ in the new retrieval analysis
(WP10). Prevalence is reported per domain so AUPRC can be read against its
baseline.

\rc{5. Ensemble with existing GRN inference methods---solid evidence.}
\textit{New experiment (WP9, cycle50).} A new Results subsection and
Table~``Geometric signal on top of expression-based GRN inference'': we computed
GENIE3-style random-forest importance and Pearson co-expression on each domain
and show that adding the geometric signal improves AUROC by $+0.023$ to $+0.085$
under edge CV, all bootstrap CIs excluding zero. The GRN-implications subsection
in the Discussion now cites this direct evidence rather than only envisioning it.

\rc{6. Can the geometric signal provide directionality? The metrics appear
symmetric.}
\textit{New experiment (WP8, cycle48).} The reviewer is correct that cosine,
centered cosine, $L_2$ and dot product are symmetric and cannot encode direction.
We added a directionality analysis using \textbf{asymmetric} per-layer features
(signed norm and mean-coordinate differences): under pair-grouped CV they predict
edge orientation at AUROC $0.80$--$0.90$, and a gene-role classifier separates
transcription factors from target-only genes at AUROC $0.74$--$0.78$. A new
Results subsection ``Asymmetric geometric features carry directional
information'' presents this; the symmetry of the main metrics is now stated
explicitly in Methods and Results.
